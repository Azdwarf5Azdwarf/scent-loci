# scent-loci

A public jar of moments keyed by smell.

The idea is small on purpose:

1. When a smell marks a moment, save the moment.
2. When a similar smell returns, fetch the old moment.
3. Let matching be fuzzy. Words, tokens, trees, and later an LLM can rummage.

This is not a machine that makes smell. Smell itself cannot be stored. What goes in the jar is a souvenir of the moment.

## What a souvenir might hold

Still an open question. Candidates from the first conversation:

- place and time (`x y z t`)
- the words used that day
- the causal tree the smell arrived as (person, habit, brand, chemicals, feeling)
- how it sat in the body
- what faded first when the nose adapted

## What this is not (yet)

- a sensor
- a perfume lab
- a finished operating system

Those can grow later. The jar has to have a door and a content before it has search.

## Open questions

- Who notices that a smell has occurred again: you, a sensor, or a model guessing from context?
- What sits at the root of the tree: the sensation, the person, the habit, the molecule, or the keypress?
- Is "locate" find-in-the-world or find-in-the-jar?
- What is being trained: the nose, the matcher, or both?
